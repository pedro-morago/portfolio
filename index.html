About

I'm Pedro, a QA engineer from Santander. I studied Mathematics at the University of Cantabria, and I think that's partly why I ended up in QA: I never liked the saying that in software, perfect is the enemy of good. Coming from maths, perfect is just perfect.

I got into software through a Java development bootcamp in 2021. When it finished, they hired me straight away as a QA engineer, because they saw I was good at it. That was five years ago.

What I enjoy most is checking that a feature really does what it should, whether by automating the tests or by catching problems early. For me, testing is about verifying functionality more than hunting for errors.

I'm very detail-oriented and I like to get to the bottom of problems until I really understand them. I use AI tools a lot in my work and I've learned to use them carefully.

I write down what I learn in my QA Wiki. The details of my work are under experience.

CI Shard Advisor

Published

Optimal parallelization of Cypress suites in CI, without relying on Cypress Cloud.

A tool that analyzes your Cypress run reports and recommends the optimal sharding strategy for your pipeline: it detects imbalances between containers and suggests the exact spec partition that minimizes total execution time, with optimality certification via a branch & bound algorithm. It's the same kind of optimization problem behind the 40% pipeline cut I delivered at IriusRisk.

  • Try it in the browser: it opens with a demo run already loaded, and you can drop in your own reports. Nothing to install, no account, and the reports never leave the browser.
  • Three interfaces: interactive web app, CLI with a quality-gate mode, and a local REST API.
  • Actionable recommendations: per-container spec lists ready to paste into GitHub Actions or Bitbucket Pipelines.
  • Pure core with hexagonal architecture, tested with property-based, metamorphic and invariant tests.

Screen Recorder (QA mode)

Published

Chrome extension for reporting bugs with full evidence in a single recording.

A browser extension (Manifest V3) that records your screen, window or tab while automatically capturing everything a developer needs to reproduce the bug: console logs synced with the video, uncaught exceptions, network requests as HAR, and numbered user steps. No time limits, no watermark, no account.

  • Install it in 30 seconds: download the zip, Load unpacked in chrome://extensions. No store account, no build step.
  • Tested against a bench I built for it: bench.pedromorago.com, a page that breaks on demand (a 500 on a write, an uncaught exception, a response that says OK and means the opposite), so the capture could be checked against known failures instead of whatever a real site happened to do.
  • Automatic step recording (clicks, forms, SPA navigations) exportable as *.steps.md. Each step carries a paste-ready Cypress or Playwright selector, so the list doubles as the skeleton of the regression test. Typed values are never logged.
  • Generates the full report and files the issue directly in Jira Cloud or Linear.
