About

I'm Pedro, a QA engineer from Santander. I studied Mathematics at the University of Cantabria and got into software through a Java bootcamp in 2021. They hired me as a QA engineer as soon as it finished, and it suited me.

What I enjoy most is checking that a feature really does what it should, whether by automating the tests or by catching problems early. I'm detail-oriented, I like to get to the bottom of a problem until I understand it, and I use AI tools a lot in my work, carefully.

I write down what I learn in my QA Wiki.

Chrome extension for reporting bugs with full evidence in a single recording.
